Start with a practical service plan

How to establish recurring fleet service.

  1. Talk through the fleet. Explain how your vehicles are used, current maintenance needs and problems your drivers have noticed.
  2. Agree on scope and scheduling. Discuss which vehicles need attention, the work requested, site access and travel charges before a visit is booked.
  3. Plan the next visit. Arrange repeat service around your operation and the maintenance needs discussed. Call separately if a breakdown needs urgent attention.

On-site service can reduce the need to move vehicles to a shop for work suitable for a mobile visit. Repair suitability and scheduling are confirmed for each job.
